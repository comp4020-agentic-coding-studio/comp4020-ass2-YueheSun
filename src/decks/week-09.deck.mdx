# Convergence

Week 9 — Comparative Literature Review due

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## What "convergence" licenses you to claim

A pattern across cases is not the same claim as an explanation for the
pattern. This week is about keeping the two visibly separate.

---

## Pooling weeks 7 and 8

This course's classification categories recur across sources with no
contact between them. Certain triggers — thresholds, repeated counts,
specific times — recur too.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# What they converge on. What they don't.

The categories and the triggers converge. Cause does not — and this
week spends as much time naming that gap honestly as it does on the
convergence itself.

---

## A first, deliberately brief look ahead

Weeks 10 and 11 take up the competing frameworks properly, including
the fourth-dimensional hypothesis. Named here only to flag that it
exists — not argued for or against yet.

---

## Why the review is marked this way

The Comparative Literature Review is marked on evaluating competing
explanations, not on picking a winner. A confident verdict this week
would outrun what the pooled corpus actually supports.
