# Case clinic 1

Week 5 — the first case with no prior write-up

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## This log is the record

No prior write-up to check against. No departmental precedent to lean on.
What you log this week is what exists.

---

## The case

The library sub-basement's stack range does not reliably return the same
floor plan between opening and closing — some weeks not at all, some
weeks completely. Aisle counts, dead ends, and the fire-door location
have all been logged differently across cohorts.

---

## Applying the scheme live

Four categories from week 2 — persistent, recurring, responsive, dormant.
This case currently reads as responsive. State why, and what evidence
would move it.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# Log it. Don't test it.

Deliberately re-entering a logged aisle to check the rearrangement is an
intervention, not an observation. It voids the log.

---

## The buddy system, tested rather than assumed

A discrepancy one observer notices is a claim. A discrepancy two
independent logs agree on is corroborated. This week, that's the entire
difference between a case and an anecdote.

---

## Sourcing discipline, turned inward

The same rule this course holds a published case to applies to a case
this course is generating itself. No exception for material closer to
home.

---

## Said plainly: no countermeasure is being tested here

This week doesn't test any of week 3's traditions. That's a real gap in
the corpus, not something to paper over.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> A log that can't be corroborated can't be classified.

---
