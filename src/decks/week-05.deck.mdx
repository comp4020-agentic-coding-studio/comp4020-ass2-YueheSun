# Case clinic 1

Week 5 — the first case with no prior write-up

---

{/* _class: impact phase-fieldwork */}

## This log is the record

No prior write-up to check against. No departmental precedent to lean on.
What you log this week is what exists.

---

## The case

Seminar Room 3, Arts Annex — flagged "do not reassign" since a fire exit
was bricked over in a past renovation. Entered alone, the room reads as
extending further than the corridor outside allows. Nobody has logged
what's at the far end, because nobody who's gone alone has found their
own way back out.

---

## Applying the scheme live

Four categories from week 2 — persistent, recurring, responsive, dormant.
This case currently reads as responsive. State why, and what evidence
would move it.

---

{/* _class: impact phase-fieldwork */}

# Never enter without a buddy staying outside

An occupant cannot find their own way back out. Only the observer who
stayed in the corridor, opening the door from that side, ends the
observation. This is not caution — it is the only way out.

---

## The case week 4 previewed

Corroboration and rescue turn out to be the same requirement. The buddy
system stops being paperwork the moment a lone observer literally cannot
leave without it.

---

## Stage one: diagnose

Thermometer and hygrometer reading, just inside the door, against a
corridor baseline. Measurably cooler and drier means the case is live
today. A normal reading means it isn't — log that too.

---

## Stage two: respond

Only once diagnosis confirms the case is live. One observer inside, one
in the corridor. Two stages, logged separately — not one blurred call
made under pressure.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> Diagnose first. Respond separately. Week 3's English witch bottles use
> the same shape — this is the first case built to test it, not just
> cite it.

---

## The nazar amulet, run honestly

Clipped to the inside door handle. Logged whether it's cracked, next to
whatever the thermometer says. Not run because anyone's sure it does
anything here — run because it costs nothing to log, and the result goes
in the file either way.
