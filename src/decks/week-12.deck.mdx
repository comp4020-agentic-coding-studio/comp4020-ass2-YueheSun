# Final debrief

Week 12 — co-taught, the Capstone is due

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## Not a recital

This isn't a list of things you memorised. It's one method, and today
is the first time you see all of it end to end.

---

## The method, in the order it was built

1. Observe under protocol — week 1
2. Classify, non-exclusively, against four categories — week 2
3. Read a countermeasure as a falsifiable claim — week 3
4. Apply it under the safety protocol — week 4
5. Write the case up so a second observer could act on it — weeks 5–6

---

## The method, continued

6. Source and compare against a wider corpus — weeks 7–9
7. Weigh it against a framework honestly, including where the
   framework doesn't fit — weeks 10–11
8. Design a protocol for a case nobody in this course has observed —
   this week

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# The Capstone was never a new skill

Every step above, once, on a case you build yourself.

---

## Idea seeds — starting points, not templates

- A handoff procedure for a case whose only failure mode is a lapse in
  observation
- A containment question for an anomaly that spreads by being copied,
  not by staying in one place
- A protocol for acting safely on a case with no single agreed account
  of itself

Picking one still means designing it.

---

## More idea seeds

- A monitoring-only protocol for a case with no active countermeasure,
  only a detection threshold
- A single protocol combining two independently-sourced week-3
  traditions, justified — including where it might not transfer
- A protocol for handling documentation itself as the hazard: who
  reviews it, how it's stored, whether watching counts as fieldwork

---

## What you actually have to trace

Not "did you use material from every week." One specific piece: a
classification category, a named countermeasure mechanism, a safety
rule — that your design would break without.

---

> A design task that uses every prior skill, not a new one.

## Your position on week 11, stated plainly

Whether and how the current leading framework shaped your protocol — an
open position, argued, not a hidden assumption carried in unexamined.
