# Applied Anomalology

Week 1 — orientation

---

## Case file, unlogged

A forum thread, three years old, still gets replies: alumni "remembering"
a hazing-week broadcast on the res-hall common-room TV — each retelling
more specific, and more wrong, than the last. Facilities confirm no such
broadcast exists in any schedule they've ever kept.

Nobody in this course is asked to explain that thread today. You're asked
to notice it's exactly the kind of thing the course takes seriously
enough to log properly — which is what the rest of this course is for.

## The eeriness was always already here

Not imported from anywhere exotic. Hidden under the surface of the campus
you already walk across — a thread like the one you just read, a
stairwell, a group chat, a doorway. This course exists to look at it
properly.

# "Applied" is not decoration

This course studies what other courses relegate to a footnote. It exists
to do something about it.

---

## The method, in three parts

1. **Observe** — log what you see, corroborate before you classify
2. **Classify** — place it in this course's scheme
3. **Countermeasure** — respond, and test the response against the next case

Every phase of the course is this method, applied to a different kind of
case.

---

## Where each phase sits

| Weeks | Phase | Method step in focus |
|-------|-------|-----------------------|
| 1–3 | Foundations | observe, classify |
| 4–6 | On-campus fieldwork | observe, under supervision |
| 7–9 | Off-campus desk study | classify, at second-hand |
| 10–11 | Literature synthesis | competing accounts of cause |
| 12 | Capstone | countermeasure, end to end |

## Fieldwork starts week 4

Everything before it is preparation for it.

---

## Before you're in the field

- an observation is voided the moment you intervene in it
- fieldwork is never run solo — a lone observer can't corroborate their own
  account
- the disengagement rule ends an observation before it costs anyone
  anything, no exceptions

You'll get the full safety protocol in week 4. Sign the paperwork before
week 5's first case clinic.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> The point is not to catalogue the anomalous for its own sake, but to
> observe it, classify it, and respond to it with a countermeasure that can
> be tested against the next case.

---

## What this course does not ask of you

- a verdict on the fourth-dimensional hypothesis, or any other contested
  framework raised in weeks 10–11
- certainty this course itself doesn't claim to have

It asks for a protocol that follows from the evidence, held to the same
standard you'll apply to every framework you read.
