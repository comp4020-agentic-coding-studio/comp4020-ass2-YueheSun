# Fieldwork safety protocol

Week 4 — before the first case clinic

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## This is procedure, not caution

Three real safety traditions converge on the same rules independently.
This course didn't invent them — it borrowed what already works.

---

## Three real traditions, one protocol

1. **Paranormal-investigation safety guidance** — never alone, sobriety
   as both a physical and observation-reliability requirement, a named
   contact with an expected return time
2. **Urban-exploration ethics** — the buddy system compared explicitly to
   scuba diving's "never dive alone," assigned roles, a deadline-based
   check-in
3. **Academic fieldwork risk-assessment policy** — a formal risk
   assessment before any fieldwork, and a distress protocol alongside the
   physical one

---

## What academic policy adds that the others don't

A plan for what happens if a team member is shaken by what they saw —
not only if they're injured. This course's disengagement rule sits
closer to that register than to a dramatic on-screen "get out now."

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# Consent is a filter, not a formality

No site is observed without the course's sign-off that it's currently
sanctioned. Nobody is placed anywhere they wouldn't otherwise go.

---

## The buddy system, justified rather than asserted

Not because two people are mechanically safer than one — because a log
backed by only one observer's word corroborates nothing, and a log that
can't be corroborated can't be classified.

---

## Next week, this stops being abstract

A case where a trapped observer cannot self-extract. Only a second
person, standing outside, can end the observation. Corroboration and
rescue turn out to be the same requirement.

---

## The disengagement rule

The moment an observer no longer feels able to leave a site at will, the
observation ends — immediately, without waiting to finish a count or
confirm a pattern.

---

> Nothing you might see in the next ten seconds is worth trading for the
> certainty that you can walk away.
