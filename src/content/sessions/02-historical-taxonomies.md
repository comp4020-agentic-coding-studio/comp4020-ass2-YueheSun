## Before the session

No preparation beyond the logging habit from week 1.

## In the session

A walk through three retired schemes and why each broke:

- an early single-axis **hostile / benign** scale, which failed the first
  time a case read as both within the same week
- a purely **spatial** scheme (indoor / outdoor / liminal), which couldn't
  place a phenomenon that changed category between observations
- the current scheme, which classifies by **behaviour under observation** —
  persistent, recurring, responsive, or dormant — rather than by appearance
  or location, and lets a case belong to more than one category at once
