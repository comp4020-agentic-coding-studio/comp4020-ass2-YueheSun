## Before the practical

Nothing to bring, and nothing yet to observe. This is the paperwork-and-
protocol week that has to happen before case clinics start next week.

## In the practical

Three rules, in the order they get applied:

1. **Consent** — every observation site is one students and staff already
   move through as part of ordinary campus life; nobody is placed anywhere
   they wouldn't otherwise go, and no site is observed without the
   course's sign-off that it is currently sanctioned for fieldwork.
2. **Buddy system** — no solo observation, ever. Not because two people are
   mechanically safer than one, but because a log backed by only one
   observer's word corroborates nothing, and a log that can't be
   corroborated can't be classified.
3. **Disengagement rule** — the moment an observer no longer feels able to
   leave a site at will, the observation ends, immediately, without waiting
   to finish a count or confirm a pattern. Nothing you might see in the next
   ten seconds is worth trading for the certainty that you can walk away.

## Afterwards

Signed consent forms and buddy pairings are due before the first case
clinic in week 5. No fieldwork happens without both on file.
