## Before the session

Bring last week's regional classifications — this week's corpus is read
against the same scheme, at a larger scale.

## In the session

The international corpus students work through this week includes cases with
no plausible route of contact between their original sources — different
countries, different eras, no shared language of description — yet a number
of them classify the same way under the department's scheme. That
repetition, not any one case's detail, is the finding the desk-study phase is
building toward.

Working through the corpus applies last week's sourcing test at volume: most
of it passes; the cases that don't are set aside from the comparison, not
deleted from the record.

## Afterwards

No submission this week. Next week's session pools this corpus with the
regional one and asks what the convergence across it does and doesn't
support.
