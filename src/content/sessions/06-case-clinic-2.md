## Before the session

Bring both cases' logs — this week reviews case clinic 1 alongside the new
case, and the Field Case File is due this week.

## In the session

The case: the west stairwell in the Design building occasionally returns an
observer to the floor they started on after descending or ascending thirteen
steps, rather than the floor the stairwell's geometry would predict. It does
not do this every time, or to every observer, or even necessarily to the same
observer twice in one week — which is most of why this course wants it
logged rather than explained.

Same protocol as case clinic 1: buddy pairs, step counts logged aloud as
they happen rather than reconstructed afterwards from memory, disengagement
the moment either observer stops trusting the count.

week's lecture reviews both cases and what a strong write-up of them looks
