## Before the session

Bring nothing except a notebook. The department's observation log template is
issued at the door — printing your own in advance defeats the point of week
one, which is calibrating everyone to the same one.

## In the session

Three exercises, each run against a short recorded excerpt from a previous
cohort's fieldwork (nothing live — that starts in week 4): log the excerpt
cold, compare notes with a neighbour before either of you has seen the
department's own transcript, then compare both against the transcript.
Discrepancies at each stage are the material, not an embarrassment.

The department's baseline protocol comes out of this:

1. **Log** what you can verify against the recording, timestamped, before you
   interpret it.
2. **Corroborate** the log against at least one other observer's before either
   of you decides what happened.
3. **Classify** only after the first two are done — never before.

Skipping the order is the single most common reason a case file gets sent back
in week 6.

## Afterwards

Nothing formal is submitted this week. The department cares whether you can
produce a log that a second person, given only the same recording, would
produce the same way — that is the standard fieldwork will hold you to from
week 4 onward.
