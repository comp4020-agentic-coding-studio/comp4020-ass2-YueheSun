## Outline

- the four categories restated as questions to ask of a case, not labels to
  memorise: does the behaviour hold steady (persistent), repeat on a
  pattern (recurring), trigger on an action (responsive), or sit inactive
  until then (dormant) — and why a case can answer yes to more than one
- worked application: an object with a genuinely affectionate, unchanging
  baseline that, rarely and only while unobserved, does something else
  entirely — classified persistent *and* dormant at once, two tracked
  channels rather than one forced fit. Full case held back for week 7;
  this week only trains the classification move, not the story behind it
- what carries forward: this vocabulary is what week 3's traditions get
  read through, and what your week 5–6 case files are marked against

## A note on this week's content

This week teaches the classification scheme on its own terms, without a
new case attached to it. The move — testing behaviour against four
questions rather than filing it under one label — is the whole lesson;
the first case sturdy enough to carry the full test doesn't appear until
week 7, once you also have the sourcing method to assess it properly.
