## Outline

- three ways a case can fail sourcing without looking obviously fake:
  a procedure that sounds official but is corroborated by nothing at all,
  an account that is structurally impossible to corroborate by design, and
  a legend with no surviving physical evidence at any point in its history
  — three distinct failure modes, not three degrees of the same one
- case-format literacy: a text write-up (a sculpture that only moves
  unobserved) and a video/software claim (a secondhand game cartridge
  whose glitches escalate) look nothing alike on the page, but the same
  three sourcing questions from the practical apply to both
- the week-2 reveal: the persistent-plus-dormant case previewed then —
  affectionate and unchanging on the surface, and, rarely, while
  unobserved, doing something else entirely — gets its full write-up this
  week, now that you have the sourcing method to read it responsibly
  rather than just find it striking
- a case's specific lesson for equipment: a secondhand cartridge whose
  glitches escalate after the "obvious fix" — wiping and resetting it —
  turns out to be the documented action that made the case worse. A
  countermeasure this course teaches you to reach for by default is not
  automatically safe against every anomaly
- Halloween candy tampering as the bridge to next week: a real,
  academically tracked legend with fewer than two dozen confirmed injuries
  across 26 years of press coverage, still driving real defensive
  behaviour every October — the clearest case this course has for
  treating a legend's effect as data even when the legend's content isn't

## Why this week has no fieldwork

The phase has changed from on-campus observation to desk study. Nothing
here was witnessed by this course directly; everything is read, sourced,
and weighed against how well it survives that reading. A striking case
that fails sourcing still gets discussed — it just doesn't get counted.

## After this lecture

This method is what week 8's global survey and week 9's convergence
analysis both depend on. A case that skips sourcing here doesn't make it
into either.
