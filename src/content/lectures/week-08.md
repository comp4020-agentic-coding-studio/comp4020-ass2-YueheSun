## Outline

- Bloody Mary: a participant, alone in a darkened bathroom, repeats a
  name a fixed number of times facing a mirror to summon a spectral
  face. The foundational scholarship's own finding — participants
  frequently report their own reflection, reframed — is this course's
  cleanest real-world case for last week's Freud citation, made concrete
  rather than asserted
- Hanako-san: a schoolgirl ghost said to haunt a specific stall of a
  school bathroom, summoned by knocking three times and asking if she's
  there. What's transmitted with unusual stability across retellings is
  the ritual's counted mechanics — which stall, how many knocks — while
  her backstory varies freely. That's backwards from most ghost lore,
  where the biography is stable and the method drifts
- read side by side, these satisfy this week's own spec line directly:
  two ritual-summoning cases, same responsive-ghost-in-an-institutional-
  fixture structure, independently documented in two countries with no
  plausible route of contact between their sources — an ordinary
  bathroom mirror and an ordinary school stall, not exotic material
  either way
- the Charlie Charlie Challenge, read for sourcing integrity rather than
  content: reported everywhere in 2015 as an authentic named demon game,
  but no such demon by that name was found by anyone who went looking.
  What actually exists is a real, older, broader Spanish-language
  pencil-balancing game family — a live example of a real tradition
  picking up a fabricated brand name and origin story during viral
  spread, the same corroboration-quality lesson week 7 opened with,
  applied across a language boundary this time

## Why the pairing matters more than either case alone

Neither case is unusual on its own — a mirror ritual and a bathroom
ghost story are both common enough to seem unremarkable. What the
pairing demonstrates is the department's actual comparative method: two
independently-sourced, no-contact cases converging on the same
structure is evidence worth weighing, and a widely-circulated account
isn't discounted just for being widely circulated — a widely-circulated
account is often exactly the kind of case with the deepest sourcing
trail to check.
