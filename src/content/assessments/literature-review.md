## The brief

> Review the regional and global case corpus and state what it does and
> does not support about a common cause behind anomalous phenomena.

The comparative method from week 7 is the review's real subject: which cases
count as evidence, and why. Breadth without evaluation is a bibliography;
evaluation without breadth is one case standing in for the whole corpus.
Neither passes here. The review is not asked to settle the fourth-
dimensional hypothesis or any other framework — that phase comes later — and
a review that reaches for a verdict it doesn't have the cases to support is
marked down, not up, for confidence.

## What you submit

A written review, any reasonable length for the case count you're handling,
citing each case with enough of its source that a marker could check it
against the comparative method's sourcing test. State the convergence you
find, and state at least one thing the corpus does not settle.
