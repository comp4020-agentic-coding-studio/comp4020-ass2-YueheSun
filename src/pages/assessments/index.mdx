Three pieces of graded work, each keyed to a specific stretch of the
semester: the Field Case File to weeks 5–6's on-campus cases, the
Comparative Literature Review to weeks 7–9's case corpus, and the Final
Capstone to the method applied whole, on a case nobody in the course has
observed. The three weights sum to 100.
