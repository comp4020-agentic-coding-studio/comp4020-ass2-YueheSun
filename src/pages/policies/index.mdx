# Policies and support

## Late work and extensions

Field observations are scheduled around a specific window (a stairwell that
behaves on Tuesdays is not guaranteed to behave on Thursdays), so case-clinic
attendance is not extendable. Written deliverables — the Field Case File,
the Comparative Literature Review, the Final Capstone — get a standard
five-working-day extension for illness or misadventure, granted on request
without needing to justify it further; ask before the deadline, not after.

## Academic integrity

Case logs must be your own observation, corroborated by your buddy pair, not
reconstructed from someone else's account. Comparing classification calls
with classmates after an observation is expected practice, not collusion —
this course wants convergent classification, not independent invention.
Submitting another observer's log as your own is treated as fabrication of
field data, this course's most serious integrity matter.

## Safety and disengagement

The disengagement rule from week 4 is not optional and is never overridden
by a desire to finish an observation. If you invoke it, you have not failed
the case clinic — you have followed the protocol correctly. Report every
invocation to the Field Safety Officer regardless of outcome.

## Getting help

Contact the Field Safety Officer for anything fieldwork-safety related, and
the convenor for anything about classification, theory, or the written
assessments. Both hold consultation hours; see the [people](/people/) page.
